PACo's atlas, stacked correlations, as look draws them: a passive or passive-active window's
virtual shot gather, its source at the window's first receiver, each trace scaled to its peak, the
time cut where 99 % of their energy has arrived, so that a clean gather's axis is short and a noisy
one's long. From PAC's test lines.

- 1. Clean (passive-active, a mute of 80 to 1,500 m/s): one sharp arrival moving out from the
  virtual source at the surface waves' speed, here about 170 m/s, and little after it.
- 2. Noisy (passive-active, unmuted): energy all along the traces and no single moveout: its image
  stripes. A mute; on a passive line, longer segments or a lower FK threshold.
- 3. Too fast for surface waves (passive, 11 receivers): an arrival crossing 15 m in about 0.014 s,
  some 1,000 m/s: body waves, or noise common to the receivers. Its lateral coherence and SNR are
  high, and its image is the smear beyond reach of image 6: the eye tells, not the measures.
