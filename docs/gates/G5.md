# G5: model QC, per window

Runs on each window's inversion (an attempt of the `inversion` stage in the QC log), from the
files the inversion leaves in the window's folder: PAC's models and forward-modelled curves,
the sampler's log, and PACo's own `SeismicInversion_Samples_0000.npz` (every posterior sample,
chain after chain). What it measured is kept next to them as
`SeismicInversion_Measures_0000.json`, for G6 and for you. Code: `src/paco/qc/g5_model.py` over
`paco.inversion.measuring`; thresholds: `model` in `qc_config.json` (`ModelThresholds`); tests:
`tests/test_qc_model.py`, and for the measures `tests/test_inversion_measuring.py`.

The monitored model is PAC's default view, the **smooth median** (sigpipe's layered median
model, smoothed with a shape-preserving spline): G5 judges its fit, and `job_status` reports it.

## What it checks

| Metric | Threshold (default) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `misfit_short`, `misfit_middle`, `misfit_long` | RMS of (picked - modelled) / uncertainty in each third of the curve's points, sorted by wavelength: at most 2.0 | About 1 is a fit within the errors; judged by band, as PAC's pseudo-section comparison shows the residual by frequency, so a model that fits the long wavelengths and misses the short ones is seen |
| `misfit_layered` | the same, over every point, for the layered median the smooth one comes from: at most 2.0 | Tells a misfit the smoothing makes from one the model makes |
| `rhat` | the largest split R-hat over the parameters (vs1, vs2, thick1, ...), each chain cut in halves: at most 1.1 | The chains sample the same distribution; Gelman's classic limit |
| `acceptance` | the lowest acceptance rate of the chains, from the sampler's log: at least 10 % | A chain that accepts nothing is stuck |
| `samples_per_chain` | models kept per chain after the burn-in: at least 100 | Fewer say nothing about the distribution |
| `at_bound` | the largest share of a parameter's samples within 2 % of its prior's range from a bound: at most 0.1 | A flat posterior puts 2 % there; five times that is a posterior pressing against the bound |
| `useful_depth` | the depth where the spread of the sampled Vs reaches half the prior's spread there (the prior drawn 2,000 times, seeded): reported, never failed | Below it the data say little; "bottom" when they inform the whole model. 0 m on 62 of the layer study's 72 models of 3 layers or more, 2.1 to 3.5 m on the other 10 (below): G6 and `job_status` use the curve's depth of investigation instead |

| Flag | Action |
|---|---|
| `not_converged` (R-hat, acceptance or samples) | sample twice as long: `inversion {"n_iterations": 2n, "n_burnin_iterations": 2b}` |
| `at_bound`, a Vs bound | widen it: the lowest by x0.8, the highest by x1.25 (`inversion {"vs_layers": [...]}`) |
| `thin_layer`, a thickness at its minimum, above 3 layers | one layer fewer (`n_layers - 1`), never below 3 |
| `deep_interface`, a thickness at its maximum | kept: the half-space would go deeper than the curve resolves (λmax/2), the limit stays |
| `smoothing_misfit`, the smooth median misfits, the layered one fits | kept: re-inverting cannot fix a smoothing effect (the decision of milestone 13) |
| `underfit`, both misfit | one more layer (`n_layers + 1`), up to the layers the curve resolves (the checks before S4: its deepest reach over its thinnest resolved layer, at least 3), at most 10 (`max_layers`); then rejected |
| `no_mode`, the layered median has no fundamental mode at some picked points | rejected, at the picking: those points are faster than any mode of a model with a softer layer below (a higher mode may be picked there) |

The cheapest fix first: `underfit` waits until the chains agree and no Vs bound holds the
posterior. The verdict is `reject` with any rejecting flag, `retry` with any change to make,
`pass` otherwise (kept flags only).

## On synthetic defects

A curve forward-modelled from a known model, 200 m/s over 350 m/s with the interface at 3 m,
9 points from 20 to 97 Hz (1.9 to 13.6 m), uncertainties of 10 % like the demo's:

| Case | Bounds | G5 | Model found |
|---|---|---|---|
| derived | Vs 148-407, thickness 0.63-6.79 | pass: misfits 0.13 / 0.26 / 0.38, R-hat 1.001, at a bound 3.9 % | 202 over 341 at 3.1 m |
| 3,000 iterations | derived | retry, `not_converged`: R-hat 1.25, 18 models a chain -> 6,000 iterations | 199 over 325 at 2.9 m |
| Vs2 at most 320 (9 % below the truth) | | pass, 6 % at the bound | 201 over 291 at 2.7 m |
| thickness at most 2 m, then 1.5 m | | pass, 6.5 % and 4.4 % at the bound | 190 over 270 at 1.7 m; 184 over 250 at 1.2 m |
| thickness at least 4 m | | pass, 8.4 % at the bound | 209 over 360 at 4.5 m |
| Vs1 at least 230 m/s | changed by the checks before S4 to 148 (above the curve's slowest velocity) | pass | 202 over 346 at 3.3 m |
| Vs2 at most 300 | | sigpipe fails (below) | |

What this says: with uncertainties of 10 %, a bound 10 to 50 % wrong still leaves a model that
fits within the errors (non-uniqueness), and the posterior piles at the bound only mildly (4 to
8 % of the samples, where the demo's own inverse windows put 5 to 9 %). G5 catches sampling too
short, and gross piles; the bounds' real protection is their derivation from the curve before
S4. With Vs2 at most 300 m/s, sigpipe's `inversion_mcmc` fails after sampling
(`operands could not be broadcast together with shapes (600,) (480,)`): one chain kept models
without their predicted curve (bayesbay stores it only once a model's likelihood has been
computed), and sigpipe sums misfits over both. PACo records the window as a failed attempt.

## Since 2026-09-25

- **PAC's residual is reported by band** (`residual_short`, `residual_middle`,
  `residual_long`: the median of (modelled - picked) / modelled, in %, of the smooth median, as
  PAC's pseudo-section comparison shows it), never judged: the misfit divides by the Lorentzian
  uncertainties, 10 to 15 % of the velocity, which make any fit look loose.
- **A `no_mode` window stays rejected, and says what to change**: the points no fundamental mode
  of the model reaches are likely a higher mode picked at high frequency, so the flag suggests
  the phase shift again with the band stopping 5 % below the lowest of them
  (`phase_shift {"dispersion": {"fmax": ...}}`), for the agent's `redo`. The job does not apply
  it (an earlier stage).

- **Never fewer than 3 layers** (the user: "4 5 6, never do 2"): the inversion starts at 4
  (`PriorRules.n_layers`), a `thin_layer` removes one down to 3 at the least, and an `underfit`
  adds one up to what the curve resolves, at most 10 (the user asked whether to start at 10 and
  remove layers: from few layers up is the usual course, since extra layers the curve cannot
  resolve leave the sampler a posterior as wide as the prior, and G5 has no measure yet telling
  a needed layer from a spurious one). The demo's table above was measured with 2 layers, PAC's
  default until then.

## The layer study (2026-09-26)

Twelve windows spread along each line, each curve inverted from 4, 5 and 6 starting layers at
PAC's effort (100,000 iterations, 5 chains), the first pass only (no G5 retry); the count is cut
to what the curve resolves (3 on the shortest curves). Script and rows:
`scratchpad/layers/` of the session (`study.py`, `results.jsonl`).

| First pass | `active_p1`, 11 receivers | `active_p2`, 5 receivers |
|---|---|---|
| Start at 4 | 12 of 12 pass; misfit short / middle / long 0.34 / 0.11 / 0.19; 68 s a window | 10 pass, 2 not converged; 0.53 / 0.13 / 0.21; 91 s |
| Start at 5 | 11 pass, 1 not converged; 0.38 / 0.13 / 0.20; 80 s | 9 pass, 3 not converged; 0.46 / 0.13 / 0.22; 105 s |
| Start at 6 | 10 pass, 2 not converged; 0.41 / 0.12 / 0.20; 75 s | 10 pass (1 at a bound, 1 not converged); 0.48 / 0.13 / 0.19; 119 s |

Every model fits within the errors in every band, at every count (misfit at most 2: 12 of 12
each time). More layers fit no better, take 15 to 30 % longer, and converge less often at the
first pass: **the inversion starts at 4** (decided by Claude while the user slept, with the
user's own lean, "better to start at 4 and then increase?": to review).

What the study also showed: with 3 layers or more, each layer's Vs posterior spans most of its
prior (xmid 8.25 of the demo: every layer 155 to 290 m/s, 5 to 95 %, in a prior of 147 to
307), while the travel-time average Vs from the surface is held to about ±15 to 20 m/s from 3 m
down. PAC's uncertainties (10 to 15 % of the velocity) let many layered models fit; the smooth
median, which averages them, is the model to read. So `useful_depth` (where the posterior's
spread reaches half the prior's) is 0 m on 62 of the study's 72 models, and 2.1 to 3.5 m on the
other 10 (all on `active_p2`, whose curves reach 4 to 19 m): it stays a reported metric, and G6
and `job_status` now use the curve's depth of investigation, half its longest wavelength
(below).

## Decided

In milestone 13 (the user accepted the thresholds): `max_misfit` 2.0 per band (1.5 rejected),
`max_at_bound` 10 % (6 % rejected: it would reject the demo's inverse windows although they
fit). On 2026-09-25: at least 3 layers, starting at 4, 5 or 6 (to be chosen from the study).
