# G5: model QC, per window

Runs on each window's inversion (an attempt of the `inversion` stage in the QC log), from the
files the inversion leaves in the window's folder: PAC's models and forward-modelled curves,
the sampler's log, and PACo's own `SeismicInversion_Samples_0000.npz` (every posterior sample,
chain after chain). What it measured is kept next to them as
`SeismicInversion_Measures_0000.json`, for G6 and for you. Code: `src/paco/qc/g5_model.py` over
sigpipe's `measure_model` (`sigpipe.masw.quality.model`: each measure of the fit to the curve,
the chains or the model, `of`; PAC's runs measured alike) and
`sigpipe.masw.inversion.measuring`; thresholds: `model` in `qc_config.json` (`ModelThresholds`
over sigpipe's `ModelLimits`); tests: `tests/test_qc_model.py`, and for the measures
`tests/test_inversion_measuring.py`.

The monitored model is PAC's default view, the **ensemble**: at each depth, the median Vs of
the kept models, with their spread. G5 judges its fit, and `job_status` reports it. PAC's
smooth median (the layered median, the kept model nearest the ensemble, smoothed with a
shape-preserving spline) fits worse: on `active_p2`, its drawn curves misfit 0.40 (median),
against 0.12 for the ensemble and 0.10 for the layered median.

The inversion is sigpipe's own: the layers chosen by the data by default (reversible jumps with
parallel tempering; `layering: "free"`), or given (DREAM(ZS); `layering: "fixed"`), a noise
factor on the picks' uncertainties sampled with the model, and a layer's Vs at most 20 % under
the one above (the priors' `max_vs_drop`). A random walk, such as bayesbay's, converges poorly
on these curves and fits little. The chains' agreement is judged on the models' Vs at five
depths the curve resolves (from a third of its shortest wavelength to a third of its longest:
the measures' `watched`), the one measure of both layerings.

## What it checks

| Metric | Threshold (default) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `misfit_short`, `misfit_middle`, `misfit_long` | RMS of (picked - modelled) / uncertainty in each third of the curve's points, sorted by wavelength: at most 2.0 | About 1 is a fit within the errors; judged by band, as PAC's pseudo-section comparison shows the residual by frequency, so a model that fits the long wavelengths and misses the short ones is seen. A band with no point to weigh (no mode, no uncertainty) is not measured, as G7's: a point without a mode is `no_mode`'s to judge |
| `residual_short`, `residual_middle`, `residual_long` | the median of (modelled - picked) / modelled, in %, in each band, as PAC's pseudo-section comparison shows it: reported | PAC's residual, never judged: the misfit divides by the Lorentzian uncertainties, 10 to 15 % of the velocity, which make any fit look loose |
| `misfit_layered` | the same, over every point, for the layered median (the kept model nearest the ensemble): at most 2.0 | Tells a misfit the median across the models makes (it blurs the interfaces they place apart) from one the model makes |
| `rhat` | the largest split R-hat of the models' Vs at the depths watched, each chain cut in halves: at most 1.1 | The chains sample the same distribution; Gelman's classic limit. A layer's own values (vs1, thick1) mean nothing when the data choose the layers, and chains that give the same profile to different layers would disagree on them |
| `samples_per_chain` | models kept per chain after the burn-in: at least 100 | Fewer say nothing about the distribution |
| `ess` | the bulk effective sample size of the models' Vs at each depth watched over the chains (rank-normalized, split chains, Geyer's initial positive sequence: Vehtari et al. 2021), the least: at least 200 | Samples that each repeat the one before say less than their number; 200 give a median and a 5 to 95 % band to a few percent. A Vs at a depth an interface may lie above or below takes two values and holds fewer than a layer's own value |
| `autocorrelation` | the chains' mean lag-1 autocorrelation of the saved samples, the largest over the depths watched: reported | What lowers the effective sample size |
| `at_bound` | the largest share of samples within 2 % of a prior's range from a bound: a parameter's (fixed layering); the top layer's or the half-space's Vs (on the logarithm, as sampled), the deepest interface, the most layers (free layering): at most 0.1 | A flat posterior puts 2 % there; five times that is a posterior pressing against the bound |
| `depth_informed` | from the kept models alone (no prior, no wavelength): from the surface down, past a top they leave open, the depth where their relative uncertainty of Vs, U(z) = (P90 - P10) / (2 P50) (from the band PAC draws on the profile), gets above 25 % but at an interface they only place at depths a little apart (in that stretch, 80 % of the models' Vs at least is each one's own just above or just below it): at least 0.8 x the depth the half-space's top may reach (fixed layering; reported only when the data choose the layers) | Below it the data say little: a model given layers reaching much deeper is shrunk to it ("bottom" when the data inform the whole model, 0 when they inform no depth). Read from the surface down, and past an interface's spike only: a layer in the stretch whose Vs they leave open ends it, though they agree again below it. G6 and `job_status` read the same depth |
| `contrast` | the smallest Vs difference between adjacent layers of the layered median, relative to their mean: judged only when the model fits, the layers given (5 %) | Two layers that alike are one; the data choosing the layers make that call themselves |

The acceptance rate is reported by the chains (their parameters file), never judged as a limit:
the samplers' moves follow the posterior (DREAM near 5 %, the reversible jumps near 25 %). When
the data choose the layers, whose steps adapt in the burn-in towards 30 % a move, the chains'
median outside 20 to 30 % is a kept flag (`acceptance`), a warning; the layers given (DREAM)
report it only.

| Flag | Action |
|---|---|
| `not_converged` (R-hat, samples or effective samples) | sample twice as long: `inversion {"n_iterations": 2n, "n_burnin_iterations": 2n/4}`, at most twice a window (`max_longer_runs`); the layers given narrow their ranges first (below) |
| `multimodal`, the chains still disagree after sampling longer twice; `few_samples`, they agree but hold few independent samples | kept, with a warning: the posterior holds several modes, and the median over the chains is less reliable (`multimodal`), or the model stands with rough uncertainties (`few_samples`). On `active_p2`, 2 of 12 windows stay at R-hat 1.3 to 2.5 up to 1.6 million iterations |
| `at_bound`, a Vs bound | widen it: the lowest by x0.8, the highest by x1.25 (`inversion {"vs_layers": [...]}`, or `{"free": {"vs_min" or "vs_max"}}` when the data choose the layers) |
| `at_bound`, the most layers allowed (free layering) | allow 2 more (`{"free": {"max_layers": n + 2}}`), at most 10 (`max_layers`); then `most_layers`, kept |
| `deep_interface` (free layering), an interface at the deepest allowed | kept: the curve resolves no deeper |
| `thin_layer`, a thickness at its minimum | one layer fewer (`n_layers - 1`), never below 3 nor the fewest the loop allows |
| `at_bound`, a thickness at its maximum | while the data inform the whole model, and the half-space stays within the curve's reach (λmax/2): widened by x1.25 |
| `deep_interface`, a thickness at its maximum at the reach | kept: the half-space would go deeper than the curve resolves |
| `uninformed`, a depth informed of 0: the models' uncertainty U over 25 % at every depth | rejected: the model is the prior's; the picks' uncertainties are too large, longer windows (a new run) give smaller ones |
| `too_deep`, the depth informed under 0.8 x the model's depth | the layers end at the depth informed (each layer's thickest shared evenly, steps in proportion), as few layers as fit; kept when even 3 layers would not |
| `smoothing_misfit`, the ensemble misfits, the layered median fits | kept: the median across the kept models blurs the interfaces they place apart, which re-inverting cannot fix |
| `underfit`, both misfit | one more layer (`n_layers + 1`), up to what the curve resolves down to the model's depth, at most 10 (`max_layers`); then rejected. When the data chose the layers, rejected: their own count did not fit |
| `alike_layers`, a model that fits with two adjacent layers under 5 % apart (layers given) | one layer fewer, never below 3 nor the fewest the loop allows |
| `strong_inversion`, a layer of the layered median under half the Vs above it (`min_vs_ratio`, 0.5) | reported, never failed: a strong low-velocity layer, possible under a stiff crust. Only an inversion whose priors let a layer's Vs drop by more than half can make one (the priors' `max_vs_drop` over 0.5; 0.2 by default, `S4_checks.md`) |
| `implausible_vs`, a layered median's Vs outside 50 to 2,500 m/s | reported, never failed |
| `no_mode`, the layered median has no fundamental mode at some picked points | rejected, at the picking: those points are faster than any mode of a model with a softer layer below (a higher mode may be picked there). The flag suggests the phase shift again with the band stopping 5 % below the lowest of them (`phase_shift {"dispersion": {"fmax": ...}}`), for the agent's `redo`; the job does not apply it (an earlier stage) |

The cheapest fix first: the layer count (`underfit`, `alike_layers`) waits until the chains
agree, no Vs bound holds the posterior and the model ends where the data inform it. A retry's
changes are one set of parameters: every flag carries the layers all of them made together.
The verdict is `reject` with any rejecting flag, `retry` with any change to make, `pass`
otherwise (kept flags only).

### The retries of a window

Each window has its own budget of 6 inversion retries, whichever asks (G5, or a failed run),
outside the run's budget. A retry whose parameters would be the attempt's before is refused,
and the window rejected (`nothing_to_try`: it would give its result again). A retry with
another layer count spreads the previous ranges over the new count and keeps the model's depth
(`relayered` in `inverting.py`), so a shrunk model stays shrunk. The loop never goes back to a
count that misfit before (`fewest_layers`): 4 layers alike -> 3, which misfit -> 4, which then
passes, never 3 again. What each window ran with is in its
`SeismicInversion_Parameters_0000.json` (the free layering's bounds as found from the curve,
each chain's acceptance, the fixed layering's typical moves); earlier attempts' files are under
`attempts/`.

A window whose curve gives no parameters (its wavelengths too few for 3 layers, see G3's
`wavelength_ratio`) is left out as a failed attempt, with why, and never retried: the line's
other windows are inverted all the same (on `active_p2`, one such curve would otherwise fail the
whole job).

### Narrow, then longer (the layers given only)

The first inversion starts wide: Vs 100 to 1,000 m/s (the half-space to 2,000), every layer 1 to
10 m thick, widened where the curve needs it. When G5 finds the chains not converged, the first
retry narrows each parameter's range to its samples' 5 to 95 %, widened by 10 % of that span and
never under 10 % of the old range (only when each chain kept at least 100 samples), with the
same iterations; later retries sample longer (at most twice).

## The layer study

Twelve windows spread along each line, each curve inverted from 4, 5 and 6 starting layers
(100,000 iterations, 5 chains), the first pass only (no G5 retry); the count is cut to what the
curve resolves (3 on the shortest curves).

| First pass | `active_p1`, 11 receivers | `active_p2`, 5 receivers |
|---|---|---|
| Start at 4 | 12 of 12 pass; misfit short / middle / long 0.34 / 0.11 / 0.19; 68 s a window | 10 pass, 2 not converged; 0.53 / 0.13 / 0.21; 91 s |
| Start at 5 | 11 pass, 1 not converged; 0.38 / 0.13 / 0.20; 80 s | 9 pass, 3 not converged; 0.46 / 0.13 / 0.22; 105 s |
| Start at 6 | 10 pass, 2 not converged; 0.41 / 0.12 / 0.20; 75 s | 10 pass (1 at a bound, 1 not converged); 0.48 / 0.13 / 0.19; 119 s |

Every model fits within the errors in every band, at every count (misfit at most 2: 12 of 12
each time). More layers fit no better, take 15 to 30 % longer, and converge less often at the
first pass: **the layers given start at 4**.

With 3 layers or more, each layer's Vs posterior spans most of its prior (xmid 8.25 of the
demo: every layer 155 to 290 m/s, 5 to 95 %, in a prior of 147 to 307), while the travel-time
average Vs from the surface is held to about ±15 to 20 m/s from 3 m down. PAC's uncertainties
(10 to 15 % of the velocity) let many layered models fit: the median across them is the model
to read.

## The depth informed

`depth_informed` comes from the inversion's results, not from the curve: the kept models'
relative uncertainty U(z) = (P90 - P10) / (2 P50) alone, the band PAC draws on the profile (its
section's uncertainty is the same U). It is the one depth informed: G6 compares the models down
to it (within the curve's depth of investigation, `G6.md`), and `job_status` reports it as
`depth_informed_m` (the curve's depth of investigation, half its longest wavelength, when the
data inform the whole model). On `active_p2`'s 90 windows of 7 receivers, a median 7.5 m
(quartiles 6.6-9.0), the whole model in 21 windows. 25 % is a practical QC limit for this
application, not a physical one.

## Design notes

- **`max_at_bound` 10 %**: at 6 % it would reject the demo's inverse windows although they fit:
  their soft half-space would go softer, below what the curves resolve, and up to 9 % of its
  samples sit at its lowest Vs.
- **The bounds' own protection is limited**: with picks' uncertainties of 10 %, a bound 10 to
  50 % wrong still leaves a model that fits within the errors (non-uniqueness), and the
  posterior piles at the bound only mildly (4 to 8 % of the samples, on a curve forward-modelled
  from 200 m/s over 350 m/s with the interface at 3 m). G5 catches sampling too short, and gross
  piles.
- **Never fewer than 3 layers**: the layers given start at 4 (`PriorRules.n_layers`), a
  `thin_layer` removes one down to 3 at the least, and an `underfit` adds one up to what the
  curve resolves, at most 10. The count grows from few layers: extra layers the curve cannot
  resolve leave the sampler a posterior as wide as the prior, and G5 has no measure telling a
  needed layer from a spurious one.
- **`min_vs_ratio`, G5's floor on a layer's Vs over the one above** (0.5), apart from the
  priors' `max_vs_drop` (0.2), which by default keeps any layer over 0.8 of the one above:
  `strong_inversion` can fire only where the priors let a layer drop by more than half.
- **A band with no point to weigh is not measured**, as G7's: its misfit is unknown, not a
  failure; a picked point without a fundamental mode is `no_mode`'s.
- A retry that would run as the attempt before is refused (`nothing_to_try`). A
  `qc_config.json` saved by an older version still reads: its `max_vs_drop` is read as
  `min_vs_ratio`.
