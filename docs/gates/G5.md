# G5: model QC, per window

Runs on each window's inversion (an attempt of the `inversion` stage in the QC log), from the
files the inversion leaves in the window's folder: PAC's models and forward-modelled curves,
the sampler's log, and PACo's own `SeismicInversion_Samples_0000.npz` (every posterior sample,
chain after chain). What it measured is kept next to them as
`SeismicInversion_Measures_0000.json`, for G6 and for you. Code: `src/paco/qc/g5_model.py` over
`paco.inversion.measuring`; thresholds: `model` in `qc_config.json` (`ModelThresholds`); tests:
`tests/test_qc_model.py`, and for the measures `tests/test_inversion_measuring.py`.

The monitored model is PAC's default view, the **ensemble**: at each depth, the median Vs of
the kept models, with their spread. G5 judges its fit, and `job_status` reports it. Until
2026-09-28 it was the smooth median (the layered median, the kept model nearest the ensemble,
smoothed with a shape-preserving spline), whose drawn curves fit 4 times worse on the user's
line (median misfit 0.40 against 0.12 for the ensemble, 0.10 for the layered median): the runs
below are of that time.

Since 2026-09-27 the inversion is sigpipe's own (bayesbay's random walk did not converge and fit
little, see PROGRESS item 38): the layers chosen by the data by default (reversible jumps with
parallel tempering; `layering: "free"`), or given (DREAM(ZS); `layering: "fixed"`), a noise
factor on the picks' uncertainties sampled with the model, and a layer's Vs at most 20 % under
the one above (`max_vs_drop`). The chains' agreement is judged on the models' Vs at five depths
the curve resolves (from a third of its shortest wavelength to a third of its longest: the
measures' `watched`), the one measure of both layerings.

## What it checks

| Metric | Threshold (default) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `misfit_short`, `misfit_middle`, `misfit_long` | RMS of (picked - modelled) / uncertainty in each third of the curve's points, sorted by wavelength: at most 2.0 | About 1 is a fit within the errors; judged by band, as PAC's pseudo-section comparison shows the residual by frequency, so a model that fits the long wavelengths and misses the short ones is seen |
| `misfit_layered` | the same, over every point, for the layered median the smooth one comes from: at most 2.0 | Tells a misfit the smoothing makes from one the model makes |
| `rhat` | the largest split R-hat of the models' Vs at the depths watched, each chain cut in halves: at most 1.1 | The chains sample the same distribution; Gelman's classic limit. A layer's own values (vs1, thick1) mean nothing when the data choose the layers, and chains that give the same profile to different layers would disagree on them |
| `samples_per_chain` | models kept per chain after the burn-in: at least 100 | Fewer say nothing about the distribution |
| `ess` | the bulk effective sample size of the models' Vs at each depth watched over the chains (rank-normalized, split chains, Geyer's initial positive sequence: Vehtari et al. 2021), the least: at least 200 (400 before 2026-09-27) | Samples that each repeat the one before say less than their number; 200 give a median and a 5 to 95 % band to a few percent. A Vs at a depth an interface may lie above or below takes two values and holds fewer than a layer's own value |
| `autocorrelation` | the chains' mean lag-1 autocorrelation of the saved samples, the largest over the depths watched: reported | What lowers the effective sample size |
| `at_bound` | the largest share of samples within 2 % of a prior's range from a bound: a parameter's (fixed layering); the top layer's or the half-space's Vs (on the logarithm, as sampled), the deepest interface, the most layers (free layering): at most 0.1 | A flat posterior puts 2 % there; five times that is a posterior pressing against the bound |
| `useful_depth` | the depth below which the spread of the sampled Vs (the interquartile range: a minority of samples in another mode would widen a standard deviation) stays at least half the prior's spread there (the prior drawn 2,000 times, seeded, of either layering), read from the bottom up so that a thin unresolved top layer does not end it: at least 0.8 x the depth the half-space's top may reach (fixed layering; reported only when the data choose the layers) | Below it the data say little: a model given layers reaching much deeper is shrunk to it ("bottom" when the data inform the whole model, 0 when they inform no depth) |
| `contrast` | the smallest Vs difference between adjacent layers of the layered median, relative to their mean: judged only when the model fits, the layers given (5 %) | Two layers that alike are one; the data choosing the layers make that call themselves |

The acceptance rate is reported by the chains (their parameters file), no longer judged: the
20 to 30 % band was a random walk's, whose steps the trial runs tuned; the new samplers' moves
follow the posterior (DREAM near 5 %, the reversible jumps near 25 %).

| Flag | Action |
|---|---|
| `not_converged` (R-hat, samples or effective samples) | sample twice as long: `inversion {"n_iterations": 2n, "n_burnin_iterations": 2n/4}`, at most twice a window (`max_longer_runs`); the layers given narrow their ranges first (below) |
| `multimodal`, the chains still disagree after sampling longer twice | kept, with the warning (the user's choice, 2026-09-26: "Cap at 2, keep with a warning"): the posterior holds several modes, the median over the chains is less reliable. On p2, 2 of 12 windows stayed at R-hat 1.3 to 2.5 up to 1.6 million iterations |
| `at_bound`, a Vs bound | widen it: the lowest by x0.8, the highest by x1.25 (`inversion {"vs_layers": [...]}`, or `{"free": {"vs_min" or "vs_max"}}` when the data choose the layers) |
| `at_bound`, the most layers allowed (free layering) | allow 2 more (`{"free": {"max_layers": n + 2}}`), at most 10 (`max_layers`); then `most_layers`, kept |
| `deep_interface` (free layering), an interface at the deepest allowed | kept: the curve resolves no deeper |
| `thin_layer`, a thickness at its minimum | one layer fewer (`n_layers - 1`), never below 3 nor the fewest the loop allows |
| `at_bound`, a thickness at its maximum | while the data inform the whole model, and the half-space stays within the curve's reach (λmax/2): widened by x1.25 |
| `deep_interface`, a thickness at its maximum at the reach | kept: the half-space would go deeper than the curve resolves |
| `uninformed`, a useful depth of 0: the posterior as wide as the prior at every depth | rejected: the model is the prior's; the picks' uncertainties are too large, longer windows (a new run) give smaller ones |
| `too_deep`, the useful depth under 0.8 x the model's depth | the layers end at the useful depth (each layer's thickest shared evenly, steps in proportion), as few layers as fit; kept when even 3 layers would not |
| `smoothing_misfit`, the ensemble misfits, the layered median fits | kept: the median across the kept models blurs the interfaces they place apart, which re-inverting cannot fix (the smooth median's smoothing, before 2026-09-28) |
| `underfit`, both misfit | one more layer (`n_layers + 1`), up to what the curve resolves down to the model's depth, at most 10 (`max_layers`); then rejected. When the data chose the layers, rejected: their own count did not fit |
| `alike_layers`, a model that fits with two adjacent layers under 5 % apart (layers given) | one layer fewer, never below 3 nor the fewest the loop allows |
| `strong_inversion`, a layer of the layered median under half the Vs above it | reported, never failed: a strong low-velocity layer, possible under a stiff crust (the user's choice, 2026-09-26: "Report, don't fail") |
| `implausible_vs`, a layered median's Vs outside 50 to 2,500 m/s | reported, never failed |
| `no_mode`, the layered median has no fundamental mode at some picked points | rejected, at the picking: those points are faster than any mode of a model with a softer layer below (a higher mode may be picked there) |

The cheapest fix first: the layer count (`underfit`, `alike_layers`) waits until the chains
agree, no Vs bound holds the posterior and the model ends where the data inform it. A retry's
changes are one set of parameters: every flag carries the layers all of them made together.
The verdict is `reject` with any rejecting flag, `retry` with any change to make, `pass`
otherwise (kept flags only).

### The retries of a window

Each window has its own budget of 6 inversion retries, whichever gate asks (G5, G6, or a failed
run), outside the run's budget. A retry with another layer count spreads the previous ranges
over the new count and keeps the model's depth (`relayered` in `inverting.py`), so a shrunk
model stays shrunk. The loop never goes back to a count that misfit before (`fewest_layers`):
4 layers alike -> 3, which misfit -> 4, which then passes, never 3 again. What each window ran
with is in its `SeismicInversion_Parameters_0000.json` (the free layering's bounds as found from
the curve, each chain's acceptance, the fixed layering's typical moves); earlier attempts' files
are under `attempts/`.

A window whose curve gives no parameters (its wavelengths too few for 3 layers, see G3's
`wavelength_ratio`) is left out as a failed attempt, with why, and never retried: the line's other
windows are inverted all the same (since 2026-09-27; on p2 one such curve had failed the whole
job).

### Narrow, then longer (since 2026-09-26; the layers given only)

The first inversion starts wide: Vs 100 to 1,000 m/s (the half-space to 2,000), every layer 1 to
10 m thick, widened where the curve needs it. When G5 finds the chains not converged, the first
retry narrows each parameter's range to its samples' 5 to 95 %, widened by 10 % of that span and
never under 10 % of the old range (only when each chain kept at least 100 samples), with the
same iterations; later retries sample longer (at most twice). The useful depth is judged
against the first attempt's prior, the reference, not against a narrowed one. The user's
choice ("Narrow, then longer"): the retries used to only lengthen the runs.

## On synthetic defects

A curve forward-modelled from a known model, 200 m/s over 350 m/s with the interface at 3 m,
9 points from 20 to 97 Hz (1.9 to 13.6 m), uncertainties of 10 % like the demo's:

| 3,000 iterations | derived | retry, `not_converged`: R-hat 1.25, 18 models a chain -> 6,000 iterations | 199 over 325 at 2.9 m |

| thickness at most 2 m, then 1.5 m | | pass, 6.5 % and 4.4 % at the bound | 190 over 270 at 1.7 m; 184 over 250 at 1.2 m |
| thickness at least 4 m | | pass, 8.4 % at the bound | 209 over 360 at 4.5 m |

| Vs2 at most 300 | | sigpipe fails (below) | |

What this says: with uncertainties of 10 %, a bound 10 to 50 % wrong still leaves a model that
fits within the errors (non-uniqueness), and the posterior piles at the bound only mildly (4 to
8 % of the samples, where the demo's own inverse windows put 5 to 9 %). G5 catches sampling too
short, and gross piles; the bounds' real protection is their derivation from the curve before
S4. With Vs2 at most 300 m/s, the sampler sigpipe used then failed after sampling
(`operands could not be broadcast together with shapes (600,) (480,)`): one chain kept models
without their predicted curve (bayesbay stored it only once a model's likelihood had been
computed). sigpipe's own chains, since 2026-09-27, keep every model's curve.

## Since 2026-09-25

- **PAC's residual is reported by band** (`residual_short`, `residual_middle`,
  `residual_long`: the median of (modelled - picked) / modelled, in %, of the smooth median, as
  PAC's pseudo-section comparison shows it), never judged: the misfit divides by the Lorentzian
  uncertainties, 10 to 15 % of the velocity, which make any fit look loose.
- **A `no_mode` window stays rejected, and says what to change**: the points no fundamental mode
  of the model reaches are likely a higher mode picked at high frequency, so the flag suggests
  the phase shift again with the band stopping 5 % below the lowest of them
  (`phase_shift {"dispersion": {"fmax": ...}}`), for the agent's `redo`. The job does not apply
  it (an earlier stage).

- **Never fewer than 3 layers** (the user: "4 5 6, never do 2"): the inversion starts at 4
  (`PriorRules.n_layers`), a `thin_layer` removes one down to 3 at the least, and an `underfit`
  adds one up to what the curve resolves, at most 10 (the user asked whether to start at 10 and
  remove layers: from few layers up is the usual course, since extra layers the curve cannot
  resolve leave the sampler a posterior as wide as the prior, and G5 has no measure yet telling
  a needed layer from a spurious one). The demo's table above was measured with 2 layers, PAC's
  default until then.

## The layer study (2026-09-26)

Twelve windows spread along each line, each curve inverted from 4, 5 and 6 starting layers at
PAC's effort (100,000 iterations, 5 chains), the first pass only (no G5 retry); the count is cut
to what the curve resolves (3 on the shortest curves). Script and rows:
`scratchpad/layers/` of the session (`study.py`, `results.jsonl`).

| First pass | `active_p1`, 11 receivers | `active_p2`, 5 receivers |
|---|---|---|
| Start at 4 | 12 of 12 pass; misfit short / middle / long 0.34 / 0.11 / 0.19; 68 s a window | 10 pass, 2 not converged; 0.53 / 0.13 / 0.21; 91 s |
| Start at 5 | 11 pass, 1 not converged; 0.38 / 0.13 / 0.20; 80 s | 9 pass, 3 not converged; 0.46 / 0.13 / 0.22; 105 s |
| Start at 6 | 10 pass, 2 not converged; 0.41 / 0.12 / 0.20; 75 s | 10 pass (1 at a bound, 1 not converged); 0.48 / 0.13 / 0.19; 119 s |

Every model fits within the errors in every band, at every count (misfit at most 2: 12 of 12
each time). More layers fit no better, take 15 to 30 % longer, and converge less often at the
first pass: **the inversion starts at 4** (decided by Claude while the user slept, with the
user's own lean, "better to start at 4 and then increase?": to review).

What the study also showed: with 3 layers or more, each layer's Vs posterior spans most of its
prior (xmid 8.25 of the demo: every layer 155 to 290 m/s, 5 to 95 %, in a prior of 147 to
307), while the travel-time average Vs from the surface is held to about ±15 to 20 m/s from 3 m
down. PAC's uncertainties (10 to 15 % of the velocity) let many layered models fit; the smooth
median, which averages them, is the model to read. So `useful_depth` (where the posterior's
spread reaches half the prior's) is 0 m on 62 of the study's 72 models, and 2.1 to 3.5 m on the
other 10 (all on `active_p2`, whose curves reach 4 to 19 m): it stays a reported metric, and G6
and `job_status` now use the curve's depth of investigation, half its longest wavelength
(below).

## Decided

In milestone 13 (the user accepted the thresholds): `max_misfit` 2.0 per band (1.5 rejected),
`max_at_bound` 10 % (6 % rejected: it would reject the demo's inverse windows although they
fit). On 2026-09-25: at least 3 layers, starting at 4, 5 or 6 (to be chosen from the study).
