# G7: petrophysical model QC, per window

Runs on each window's petrophysical inversion (an attempt of the `petro_inversion` stage in the
QC log), which the agent starts only when the user asks for soils or the water table
(`invert_petro`). A Silex model predicts the window's soil column (soil types, SPT N values,
the water table) from its picked fundamental mode; santiludo's rock physics turns the column
into Vp, Vs and density, and disba gives back its curve. What G7 measured is kept next to PAC's
files as `PetroInversion_Measures_0000.json`, for G8 and for you. Code:
`src/paco/qc/g7_petro.py` over `sigpipe.masw.petro.measuring`; thresholds: `petro` in
`qc_config.json` (`PetroThresholds`); tests: `tests/test_qc_petro.py`.

The user's decision (2026-09-26): "Range, fit, line". Before G7, the **range check**: a curve
outside the chosen model's trained band and velocities, beyond Silex's own 20 % margin, is not
inverted, and the summary says how the curves fall outside. The bundled model,
`grand_est_15-50hz_193-415mps`, needs a curve starting at 22 Hz at the latest and ending at
43 Hz at the earliest, between 149 and 459 m/s.

## What it checks

| Metric | Threshold (default) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `misfit_short`, `misfit_middle`, `misfit_long` | RMS of (picked - given back) / uncertainty in each third of the curve's points, sorted by wavelength: at most 2.0 | G5's measure and limit: about 1 is a fit within the errors. A band with no point to weigh is judged by the others |
| `residual_short`, ... | the median (given back - picked) / given back, % | PAC's residual: reported, never judged |
| `water_table` | the model's water table, m | reported |

| Flag | Action |
|---|---|
| `misfit`, a band above the limit | rejected: the model's soil column does not give the curve back. The message names the other bundled models covering the curve (`invert_petro` with one of them), or says none does and to check the pick in PAC |
| `no_mode`, the column's curve has no fundamental mode at some picked points | rejected, with the same advice |
| `no_uncertainty`, no picked point carries an uncertainty | rejected, at the picking: the fit cannot be weighed |

No retry: a model predicts one soil column per curve, so running it again changes nothing. The
verdict is `reject` with any flag, `pass` otherwise. A window G7 rejects is left out of G8 and
of the sections.

A picked point without an uncertainty (NaN, or 0) weighs nothing in the misfit (sigpipe's
`fit_by_band`, G5's too): the agent's picks can end on one, which made the whole band's misfit
NaN.

## On the demo profile

`active_p1`'s curves are short for this model: most end below the 43 Hz it needs.

- **24-receiver windows every 12** (the tests, 2026-09-26): 6 curves G4 passed. The model
  covers 1: xmid 5.88. G7 passes it, misfit 0.43 / 0.17 / 0.35 by band, residual 3 / 4 / 9 %.
  Its column is clay over loam over sand, 17, 3 and 1 m thick, N 6, with the water table at
  2 m.
- **The agent's 11-receiver windows**, from its run in PAC: 74 curves G4 passed, ending at 32
  to 44 Hz (median 37 Hz), and the model covers 5. Their misfit over all points is 0.45 to
  1.85. G7 rejects one of them, whose short band misfits 2.2.
- **48-receiver windows every 24:** 3 curves G4 passed, and the model covers 1.

On `active_p2`, the real line, the model covers 6 of the 85 curves G4 passed. Of the others,
65 end below 43 Hz, 10 are faster than 459 m/s and 5 start above 22 Hz. G7 passes all six,
misfit 0.01 to 0.71 by band.

The limit stays G5's until models trained on other bands, or lines whose curves reach the
model's band, say otherwise (rule 9).
