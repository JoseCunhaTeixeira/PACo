# G1: signal QC, per record

Runs on each preprocessed record (`records/<record>/Stream_0000.hdf5`), after S1 (trigger
correction, detrend, mute, filter). On a passive record, only the checks that need no trigger:
dead, clipped and NaN traces, and the spectra (below). Code: `src/paco/qc/g1_signal.py`, which
judges the measures of sigpipe's `measure_signal` (`sigpipe.masw.quality.measures`, PAC's runs
measured alike); thresholds: `signal` in `qc_config.json` (`SignalThresholds`, over sigpipe's
`SignalLimits`); tests: `tests/test_qc_signal.py`, on synthetic shots with one defect planted
at a time, and sigpipe's `tests/masw/test_quality_signal.py` for the measures.

## What it checks

The surface-wave window of each trace runs from the arrival at `vg_max` to the arrival at
`vg_min` (80 to 1,500 m/s by default), padded by 50 ms. The noise window is before the trigger
when the record has samples there, else after the slowest arrival on every trace when the
record is long enough, else after the slowest arrival on the traces within the line's reach
(below); with room for neither, the record is left out (`record_too_short`). Each measure says
what it describes (`of`: the signal, or its spectrum) and what it covers (`over`: the traces,
the band, where the noise was); PAC shows them in a menu for each. On a muted record, the SNR, the
usable band, the first breaks and the shot's pulse are measured on the record before its
muting, and so is the line's reach; the coherence on the muted record, as the windows use it
(below).

| Metric | Threshold (default) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `dead_traces` | RMS below 1 % of the record's median RMS | A dead geophone: no parameter improves it |
| `clipped_traces` | more than 0.5 % of the samples at the trace's extreme | Saturation: the waveform is lost |
| `nan_traces` | any NaN | Unusable |
| `rms_outliers` | log RMS off the Theil–Sen fit of log RMS against log offset by more than 3 MADs **and** a factor 2; the fit runs on every trace within the line's reach (below) that is neither dead, clipped nor NaN, those already excluded included | Bad coupling; the factor keeps a smooth decay's tiny MADs from flagging everything. Without the excluded traces, the fit moves at each round and excludes more (21 traces around each shot of `active_p2`); over a long line's noise, it flattens, and the strongest traces next to the shot read too loud |
| `snr_db` | median over the traces within the line's reach (below) of the energy per sample in the signal window over the noise window, in the part of the usable band the dispersion images use (the usable band capped to the preset's dispersion band, `dispersion.fmin` to `fmax`: 0 to 100 Hz on `active_p2`), as a filter to it would give it: at least 6 dB (`min_snr_db`) | Below it the image is mostly noise: the record is left out at once (`low_snr`). A filter common to the traces cannot change a phase-shift image, which divides each trace's spectrum by its own amplitude, and the noise is measured before any mute: no retry could change the measure. Traces beyond the reach go into no window's stack: a long line's far traces are not the record's fault |
| `usable_band_hz` | the band, around the spectral peak, where the signal windows' spectrum exceeds the noise windows' by 6 dB and stays within 20 dB of its peak among those frequencies, over the traces within the reach; both spectra at one resolution, each trace's noise window cut in pieces as long as its surface-wave window (half overlapping, each Hann-tapered, averaged); the record's whole usable band, which must reach at least 2 Hz (`min_band_hz`) into the band the images use | What S2's band may span. Under 2 Hz there is no dispersion to see and no band to measure the SNR in: the record is left out at once (`no_usable_band`). The peak rule is needed because after the wave the noise window is quieter than the signal window at every frequency; at one resolution, a hum's line, as loud in the noise, fakes no band (the short surface-wave window alone would spread it over its sides) |
| `lateral_coherence` | median over neighbouring pairs within the reach of the peak normalized cross-correlation in the window, within the moveout of one spacing at `vg_min`, in the band the SNR is measured in: at least 0.5 | Noisy or scattered records: left out at once (`low_coherence`), as for the SNR |
| `trigger_error_s` | first breaks (envelope smoothed over 5 ms above 5 x the noise RMS, on traces whose SNR passes) of the 6 traces nearest the shot (`trigger_traces`), on the record before its muting, fitted as t = t0 + x / v (Theil–Sen): abs(t0) at most 10 ms (`max_trigger_error_s`), judged on a muted record only | A delay the same on every trace is corrected in S1 (`trigger.t0`, the muting's trigger). The nearest traces' first arrival is the direct wave, whose line runs through the shot; farther, a refractor's head wave arrives first, and its line crosses the shot's time at its intercept time, no trigger delay. Not muted, a delay common to the traces changes no image: reported |
| `trigger_scatter_s` | 1.4826 x the MAD of every break about a line through them all, at most 50 ms | Beyond it the breaks are no line: the record is rejected |

**The line's reach** (sigpipe's `line_reach`, measured by `process_line` before G1 judges the
records; PAC's runs are measured within it alike): each trace's SNR (the same windows, over the
whole spectrum, each record before its muting) over every active record, binned by distance
from the shot every fifteenth of the line; the reach is where the bins' median falls under
`reach_snr_db` (2 dB), interpolated between the last bin above and the first below. It sets
S2's `masw.distance_max` unless the user gave one (the windows stack no farther shot), and G1
judges the decay, the SNR, the usable band and the coherence within it. None on a passive line,
or when no bin falls under 2 dB.

Actions: exclude traces (dead, clipped, NaN; a trace off the decay is the line's to leave out,
below), exclude the record (no noise window; no usable band, or the SNR or the coherence under
its limit; an inconsistent trigger), an override of the preprocessing (`trigger.t0`, a muted
record's delay). No filter or mute is asked: neither could change what is measured (below).
Verdict: `reject` when a flag excludes the record, `retry` when a flag can be acted on, `pass`
otherwise. `kept`: the usable band and the traces kept. G1's retries draw on each record's own
budget, not the windows' run budget (`loop.md`). A record still asking for a retry once its
budget is spent ("budget spent"), or whose retry would run as the attempt before
(`nothing_to_try`), is rejected and goes into no window. A `qc_config.json` saved by an older
version still reads: its `max_trigger_shift_s` is read as `max_trigger_error_s`.

## The line's receivers

A trace off the amplitude decay with offset in one record is reported, not left out: next to
the shot, such traces are artifacts of the shot, not bad receivers (about 40 % of those found
on `active_p2`). A receiver is left out of every window only when it is off the decay in at
least half the records that reach it, and in at least 3 of them: a line-level check, unit
`line`, flag `off_decay_receivers`. On `active_p2`: the receiver at 45 m, off in 37 of its 69
records (a badly coupled geophone). The near field leaves no trace out: it sets the nearest
shot a window stacks (`S2_rules.md`).

## On the demo profile

`active_p1`'s records start 20 ms before the shot: their SEG-2 headers say `DELAY -0.020`, and
the first break on the 0.75 m trace comes at 19 to 21 ms, where a direct wave takes about 1 ms.
The muting moves each record's time origin by that delay (below). The traces of 2.dat off the
decay, the two nearest the shot among them (2.0 and 2.25 m, in the near field), are reported,
not left out: the line has two records, fewer than the 3 a receiver must be off in.

## On a real survey

`active_p2`: 96 receivers every 1.5 m (142.5 m), 97 shots from -0.75 to 143.25 m every 1.5 m,
3 s at 2,000 Hz. The traces' median SNR falls from 53 dB within 10 m of the shot to 7 dB at 40
to 50 m, 5 dB at 50 to 60 m, about 0.5 dB beyond 70 m. The reach at 2 dB is 63.35 m.

- Judged over whole records, 52 of the 97 records have a median SNR under 6 dB: their near
  traces are strong, most of the line is noise. Within the reach, 94 pass and 3 are rejected.
- A reversed-polarity check flags blocks of traces next to every shot: at 1.5 m spacing near
  the source, neighbours shifted by more than half a period correlate negatively. G1 has none.
- A decay fit without the traces it excludes moves at each round, and excludes 21 traces
  around each shot: the fit keeps them.
- Fitted over all 96 traces, most of them noise, the decay flattens, and the traces next to
  each shot, the strongest (up to 73 dB), read too loud: 360 traces off the decay, 259 of them
  within 10 m of the shot. Within the reach: 131, 70 within 10 m (mostly the nearest one or
  two, the near field), and receiver 30, loud on most shots, still found (38 records).
- The records' usable bands: the median 2.3 to 298 Hz; the worst record's 24 to 86 Hz, which
  would cut the whole line's long wavelengths as the band's cap (`S2_rules.md`).

## On synthetic shots (the tests)

A causal wavelet at 200 m/s over white noise, 24 traces 1 m apart:

- clean: pass, SNR 27 dB in its usable band (0 to 44 Hz; 16 dB over the whole spectrum),
  coherence 1.00, the trigger 0.4 ms off, reported (not muted);
- a zeroed trace, a clipped trace, a NaN: found and excluded, verdict retry;
- a trace 30 times louder: the only trace off the decay, reported, not left out;
- noise of RMS 0.5 against a wave peaking at 1: 5.5 dB in its usable band (9.5 to 30 Hz),
  `low_snr`, left out at once; of RMS 1: `no_usable_band`, left out;
- a shot 50 ms late, muted with its trigger at 0: its first breaks, on the record before its
  muting, give `shifted_trigger` with t0 = 0.050, and the record corrected by that t0 passes;
  without the record before its muting no break is taken (on the muted record they would be the
  mute's edge); not muted, the 50 ms are reported, not judged;
- each trace shifted by its own -150 to 150 ms: `inconsistent_trigger`, rejected.

## The trigger, part of the muting

sigpipe reads each record's trigger from its file (SEG-2's `DELAY`, optional: a file may not say
it); the demo's records and `active_p2`'s all say 20 ms, the delay the first breaks find. The
trigger belongs to the muting: with the muting on, the preprocessing moves each record's time
origin by it (or by a `trigger.t0` given; 0 for a file that says none); off, it leaves the
record as recorded. So G1 expects the shot at 0 on a muted record, and where its file says on
the others: `trigger_error_s` is the first breaks' shot time off that (1.dat: 18.8 ms measured
against 20 ms, -1.2 ms). Only a muted record's trigger is corrected (`shifted_trigger`, its t0
the shift applied plus the one measured, never below 0); unmuted, nothing uses it, and the
measure is only reported.

The shot's pulse (`pulse_s`, reported): how long its energy lasts after the first break, where
the envelope falls back below a tenth of its peak (`pulse_ratio`), the median over the three
traces nearest the shot that show one (`pulse_traces`), within half a second (`max_pulse_s`).
Measured on a record not muted, or on a muted one before its muting (a mute would cut it); on
the demo, 0.05 to 0.15 s (`active_p2`'s first twelve shots, median 0.08 s). Every mute a gate
suggests leaves its width out: when the records are done again (G1's own retries, a step back:
the agent's, or PACo's at the end of `pick`, `loop.md`), each record's mute keeps its own pulse
after the slowest arrival, else the line's median pulse, else 50 ms (`mute_width_s`) when no
record's could be measured (`shots.py`). A width the user gives stays.

## Each record's spectra

On every line (`SPECTRA_MODES`), each record's traces against their neighbours' spectra
(sigpipe's `spectral_deviations`: Welch's spectra, two neighbours each side, 2 Hz to 0.8 of
Nyquist): a trace sitting more than 6 dB from theirs (the median of the differences: a gain or
a response of its own), or dropping 15 dB under them over more than a tenth of the band (a dead
band). A noise record's traces are compared by level and shape; a shot's (active or
passive-active) by shape alone, the five nearest the shot aside (louder and brighter by their
distance alone). Each record reports how many are off (`spectral_outliers`); the line flags a
receiver off in half the records judging it or more (`spectral_receivers`), and keeps it: a
flag, never a change to the workflow. On the demo, a passive trace sits 1 dB from its
neighbours (3.4 at the 99th percentile), a shot's 1.7 (5.6); `active_p2`'s receiver 20 stands
out in several shots (4.7 to 7.3 dB). An active run saved by an older version may hold no
spectrum measure.

Each record's spectra are drawn beside it when G1 judges it (`Spectrum_0000.png`, archived
with the record's attempt): the preprocessed record's traces, each trace's amplitude spectrum
at its receiver scaled to its own largest, 0 to Nyquist, bone reversed; G1's usable band
dashed, no title.

## The noise before the muting

A muting zeroes the noise window after the slowest arrival: measured there, a muted record's
median SNR reads some 360 dB, whatever its noise (four muted records of `active_p2`; 352 dB on
a passive-active run in PAC). So on a muted record, the SNR, the usable band and the line's
reach measure the noise on the record before its muting: preprocessed as its latest attempt
had it, from its input file, neither muted nor its trigger shifted (the shift, part of the
muting, drops the samples before the trigger), its shot later by that shift (sigpipe's
`unmuted_record`, `trigger_shift_s`; `judging.before_muting`). The first breaks and the pulse
are read there too. The other measures stay on the muted record, as the windows use it: dead,
clipped and NaN traces, the decay, the coherence. Measured before the muting, three of those
four records would fall under the coherence limit (0.48 to 0.50, against 0.51 to 0.56 muted).

On a run of `active_p2` (20260927-094830-487e: 97 records, the line's reach 63.35 m), the four
muted records measured both ways; no verdict changes:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 117.dat | 364.8 dB | 19.2 dB | 0-306 Hz | 14-200 Hz | pass |
| 120.dat | 364.1 dB | 12.7 dB | 0-285 Hz | 14-208 Hz | pass |
| 121.dat | 364.2 dB | 15.9 dB | 0-325 Hz | 14-266 Hz | retry (coherence 0.49) |

## Design notes

G1 measures what the dispersion images are made of, and asks for no retry that could not
change its measure.

- **A record under its limits is left out at once** (`no_usable_band`, `low_snr`,
  `low_coherence`), with no filter or mute to try. The phase shift divides each trace's
  spectrum by its own amplitude, so a filter common to the traces cannot change the image: the
  SNR and the coherence are measured in the band, as a filter to it would give them, and the
  noise before the muting, which a mute cannot change. Such retries measure the same: on a run
  of `active_p2`, they spend 12 attempts on records left out all the same.
- **In the part of the usable band the images use**: on `active_p2`, the usable band reaches
  240 to 270 Hz from energy next to the shot that no image uses (the images stop at 100 Hz),
  and on which neighbouring traces agree less. Over two runs of that line, 9 records have a
  coherence of 0.51 to 0.60 in 0 to 100 Hz, most of which would fall under the limit over the
  whole spectrum. `usable_band_hz` stays the record's whole usable band, which must reach 2 Hz
  into the images' band (`min_band_hz`: a frequency or two show no dispersion).
- **The trigger from the direct wave, before the muting**: on the muted record, the first
  breaks are the mute's edge. The trigger is fitted on the 6 traces nearest the shot
  (`trigger_traces`: the direct wave; farther, a refractor's head wave arrives first, its line
  crossing the shot's time at its intercept time, no trigger delay), the scatter over every
  break. On a run of `active_p2` (20260928-153714-f215), 57 records are about 11 ms off. Judged
  on a muted record only: unmuted, a delay common to the traces changes no image.
- **The noise window after the slowest arrival on every trace when the record is long enough,
  else on the traces within the reach**: on every trace alone, a long line's end shots, whose
  far traces the slowest wave reaches after the record ends, would be too short for offsets no
  window stacks; on the reach's traces alone, the window takes in the coda of records long
  enough, and leaves out 105 and 107.dat of `active_p2`.
- **The usable band at one resolution** (above): the long noise window resolves a hum's line
  sharply, where the short surface-wave window spreads it over its sides, which then read as
  signal.
- **10 ms for a trigger correction, 50 ms for "not a line"**, and no lower edge to the usable
  band: the pick stops where its ridge breaks (8.5 to 10 Hz at the lowest on the demo).
- **The near field stays in the decay fit**: the longest wavelength a curve keeps is not known
  at G1, before any image. The record's dominant wavelength (the phase velocity at its
  spectrum's peak) is 6.2 m on 1.dat and 1.8 m on 2.dat, whose spectrum peaks high next to the
  shot: its near field (0.9 m) misses traces 89 and 90 (2.0 and 2.25 m), the case such a rule
  is for, and on 1.dat leaving the near traces out of the robust fit flags trace 62 instead. Two
  traces of 96 cost little; G3's `near_field` reports the near field's effect on the curves.
- **G1's retries count against each record's own budget**, not the windows' run budget: a line
  of 97 shots would spend the run's budget on records before any window.
- **The SNR and the usable band within the line's reach**, the reach at 2 dB of median SNR
  setting `masw.distance_max`. Not PAC's 100 m: `active_p2`'s traces are noise beyond about
  60 m. Not every shot of the line: a long line's far traces are noise. Not 6 dB or 3 dB for
  the reach: at 6 dB the demo's end windows lose their far shot.
- **The decay fitted within the reach**, as the coherence: over a long line the far noise
  flattens the fit.
- **No reversed-polarity check**: on `active_p2` it flags the traces next to each shot, whose
  neighbours are shifted by more than half a period. A geophone wired backwards would show as a
  trace G2 and G3 cannot explain; neither line has one.
