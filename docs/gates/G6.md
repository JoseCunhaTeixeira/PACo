# G6: model profile QC, over the line

Runs once over the whole line after the inversions, on the smooth median of every window whose
latest inversion passed G5 (read back from its `SeismicInversion_Measures_0000.json`). Code:
`src/paco/qc/g6_models.py`, called by `judge_model_line` in `inverting.py`, on the side
comparison it shares with G4 (`sides.py`); thresholds: `models` in `qc_config.json`
(`ModelProfileThresholds`); tests: `tests/test_qc_model_profile.py`, on synthetic lines.

## What it checks

The section Vs(depth, xmid) at round depths down to half the line's median longest wavelength
(1 to 5 m on the demo), each model down to its own useful depth: its curve's depth of
investigation, half its longest wavelength (since 2026-09-26; the posterior's useful depth of
G5 is 0 m on most models of 3 layers or more, `G5.md`). Each model is compared with
two neighbours a side, as G4 compares the curves: it fits a side within `max_misfit` (median
relative difference of Vs over the depths they share), it is off a side only when that side
holds two models that agree with each other.

| Situation | Flag | Action |
|---|---|---|
| off agreeing neighbours, fits no side, while its curve fits theirs (G4 raised nothing on it) | `non_unique` (retry) | another model fits the same data: invert again, sampling twice as long |
| the same, and G4 found the curve a change shared with one side | `model_change` (keep) | the curves show the change too |
| the same, and the curve had no agreeing neighbours in G4 | `model_change` (keep) | the curves cannot tell |
| off one side, fits the other | `model_change` (keep) | a change in the ground shared with the neighbours |
| fits no side, off none | `no_neighbours` (keep) | nothing to judge it against |

The line gets its own result (unit `line`): the xmids without a model as `gaps`, and
`uneven_useful_depth` when the depths of investigation spread by more than
`max_useful_depth_spread` (MAD over median) along the line; rejected only without any model. No lateral smoothing, neither
by editing models nor by using neighbours as priors: the spec forbids it, since it would create
the smoothness G6 then reports.

| Threshold | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `neighbours` | 4 (2 a side) | As G4 |
| `max_misfit` | 0.15 | The demo's models sit within 0.4 to 19.5 % of their neighbours' (below); a model 30 % off its agreeing neighbours is an outlier |
| `min_shared_depths` | 2 | One depth says nothing about a model |
| `max_useful_depth_spread` | 0.5 | The demo's useful depths spread by 10 % |

## On synthetic lines (the tests)

Models Vs = 200 + 20 z at 1 to 5 m, eight xmids:

- all alike: every model and the line pass;
- one model 30 % faster while its curve fits its neighbours': `non_unique`, invert again with
  twice the iterations; its neighbours pass;
- the same model when G4 found its curve a shared change: `model_change`, kept ("the curves show
  the same change"); when its curve had no agreeing neighbours: kept ("the curves cannot tell");
- a step of 50 % from the fifth xmid on: `model_change` on the two models at the step, kept;
- gaps at xmids 6 and 7, and useful depths of 5 m on half the line and 1.5 m on the other:
  `gaps` and `uneven_useful_depth`; a model informed to its bottom counts at its deepest depth;
  no model at all: the line rejected.

## Decided

In milestone 13 (the user accepted the thresholds): `non_unique` re-inverts with twice the
iterations (one more layer rejected), `max_misfit` 0.15 on Vs (0.2 rejected). Kept as they are
(the user, 2026-09-25): the models compared by Vs down to their useful depth, not by their
interface depths. Since 2026-09-26 (Claude, while the user slept: to review) that depth is the
curve's depth of investigation: with the 4 layers the inversion now starts from, the posterior's
useful depth left G6 no depth to compare ("No model at xmid 2.88-20.88" with two models that
passed G5).
