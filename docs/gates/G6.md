# G6: model profile QC, over the line

Runs once over the whole line after the inversions, on the ensemble of every window whose
latest inversion passed G5 (read back from its `SeismicInversion_Measures_0000.json`). Code:
`src/paco/qc/g6_models.py`, called by `judge_model_line` in `inverting.py`, on the side
comparison it shares with G4 and G8 (sigpipe's `sigpipe.masw.quality.line`); thresholds:
`models` in `qc_config.json` (`ModelProfileThresholds`); tests:
`tests/test_qc_model_profile.py`, on synthetic lines.

## What it checks

The section Vs(depth, xmid) at round depths down to half the line's median longest wavelength
(1 to 5 m on the demo), each model down to its depth informed, G5's (where the kept models'
uncertainty U stays under 25 %; below it the priors speak, not the data), within its curve's
depth of investigation, half its longest wavelength (`G5.md`). Each model is compared with two
neighbours a side among the windows within `max_gap_steps` of the line's steps, as G4 compares
the curves: it fits a side within `max_misfit` (median relative difference of Vs over the
depths they share), it is off a side only when that side holds two models that agree with each
other. The measure is `neighbour_misfit`, the largest misfit to a side compared with.

| Situation | Flag | Action |
|---|---|---|
| off agreeing neighbours, fits no side, while its curve fits theirs (G4 raised nothing on it) | `non_unique` (keep) | another model fits the same data: kept, to look at. Its chains agree (G5 passed it converged), so sampling longer would draw the same posterior again |
| the same, and G4 found the curve a change shared with one side | `model_change` (keep) | the curves show the change too |
| the same, and the curve had no agreeing neighbours in G4 | `model_change` (keep) | the curves cannot tell |
| off one side, fits the other | `model_change` (keep) | a change in the ground shared with the neighbours |
| fits no side, off none | `no_neighbours` (keep) | nothing to judge it against |

The line gets its own result (unit `line`): the xmids without a model as `gaps`, and
`uneven_depth_informed` when the models' depths informed (as compared: a model informed to its
bottom counts at its deepest compared depth) spread by more than `max_depth_informed_spread`
(MAD over median) along the line (`depth_informed_spread`); rejected only without any model. No
lateral smoothing, neither by editing models nor by using neighbours as priors: the spec forbids
it, since it would create the smoothness G6 then reports.

| Threshold | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `neighbours` | 4 (2 a side) | As G4 |
| `max_misfit` | 0.15 | The demo's models sit within 0.4 to 19.5 % of their neighbours'; a model 30 % off its agreeing neighbours is an outlier |
| `min_shared_depths` | 2 | One depth says nothing about a model |
| `max_gap_steps` | 3 | Windows across a gap in the line are not neighbours (as G4's and G8's) |
| `max_depth_informed_spread` | 0.5 | Beyond it the models do not see equally deep |

## On synthetic lines (the tests)

Models Vs = 200 + 20 z at 1 to 5 m, eight xmids:

- all alike: every model and the line pass;
- one model 30 % faster while its curve fits its neighbours': `non_unique`, kept, pass; its
  neighbours pass;
- the same model when G4 found its curve a shared change: `model_change`, kept ("the curves show
  the same change"); when its curve had no agreeing neighbours: kept ("the curves cannot tell");
- a step of 50 % from the fifth xmid on: `model_change` on the two models at the step, kept;
- four models, a gap of six steps, four others 50 % stiffer: no flag (across the gap they are
  no neighbours);
- gaps at xmids 6 and 7, and depths informed of 5 m on half the line and 1.5 m on the other:
  `gaps` and `uneven_depth_informed`; a model informed to its bottom counts at its deepest
  depth; no model at all: the line rejected.

## Design notes

G6 asks for no retry that could not change the model, reads one depth informed, and compares
near neighbours only.

- **`non_unique` is kept, with its flag**: G5 passed the model converged, its chains agreeing
  on one posterior, and sampling it twice as long draws the same posterior again.
- **The models are compared by Vs down to G5's depth informed**, within the curve's depth of
  investigation, not by their interface depths: below the depth informed the priors speak, and
  models differ by them alone. The line's spread is of the same depths
  (`depth_informed_spread`, `max_depth_informed_spread`, `uneven_depth_informed`).
- **Neighbours within 3 of the line's steps** (`max_gap_steps`), as G8's: models across a gap in
  the line say nothing of each other. The measure is `neighbour_misfit`: "misfit" alone names a
  fit's misfit to its curve (G5's, G7's).
- A `qc_config.json` saved by an older version still reads: its `max_useful_depth_spread` is
  read as `max_depth_informed_spread`.
