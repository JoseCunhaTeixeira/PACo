# G8: petrophysical profile QC, whole line

Runs after G7, over the windows whose petrophysical model G7 passed. It compares each window
with its neighbours on either side, the sides of G4 and G6 (`sigpipe.masw.quality.line`). The
compared values are the Vs of the window's rock physics at the line's report depths, down to its
curve's depth of investigation, and its water table. Only windows within a few of the line's
steps count as neighbours: the range check leaves gaps, and models far apart say nothing of
each other. Code: `src/paco/qc/g8_petro_line.py`;
thresholds: `petro_line` in `qc_config.json` (`PetroLineThresholds`); tests:
`tests/test_qc_petro.py`.

## What it checks

| Metric | Threshold (default) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `misfit` | the median relative difference of the rock physics' Vs to a side's median, over the depths they share: at most 0.15 | G6's limit, on the same depths |
| `water_table_jump` | the difference of the water table's depth to a side's median: at most 1 m | One of the model's steps (it was trained on 1 to 10 m, by 1 m). In metres, not relative: 0.5 m against 1.5 m was 67 % on `active_p2` |
| neighbours | the windows within 3 of the line's steps (`max_gap_steps`) | On `active_p2`, compared windows were 75 m apart |
| `models` (line) | windows with a model G7 passed: at least 2 | A section needs two |
| `water_table_min`, `water_table_max` (line) | reported | |

| Flag | Action |
|---|---|
| `petro_outlier`, off neighbours that agree, while the window's curve fits theirs (G4) | rejected: left out of the sections. No retry: a model gives one column per curve |
| `petro_change`, off its neighbours, and the curves show the change too (G4's `shared_change`) | kept: a change in the ground |
| `gaps` (line), windows with no model G7 passed | kept: the sections have gaps there |

PAC's sections (soils and N, shear modulus, Vs, each a figure and an HDF5 file) are written over
the windows G7 and G8 passed.

## On the demo profile

With one model covered on `active_p1` (G7.md), G8 passes the window, which has no neighbour to
compare with, and rejects the line: fewer than two models, no section. The summary the agent
reads:

```
Silex model grand_est_15-50hz_193-415mps covers 1 of the 6 curves G4 passed.
Left out, outside its range: 5 end below 43 Hz.
The model G7 and G8 passed: soils by thickness clay 81%, loam 14%, sand 5%; water table 2 m deep.
No section: fewer than two models passed.
G7: 1 pass
G8: 1 pass, 1 reject
G8 gaps, line: No petrophysical model at xmid 2.88 (1), 8.88-20.88 (5): the sections have gaps
  there. -> keep: the gaps stay in the report
```

On `active_p2`, the real line (windows of the length the data chose, every 1.5 m, 85 curves G4
passed), the model covers 6 curves, at xmid 6, 15, 25.5, 100.5, 124.5 and 130.5. G7 passes all
six (misfits 0.01 to 0.71 by band). No two are within 3 steps (4.5 m) of each other, so G8 has
nothing to compare and passes them. PAC's six section files are written: sand 66 % of the
soils' thickness, clay 21 %, silt 11 %, loam 2 %, and the water table 0.5 to 2 m deep.

The first version compared the nearest windows at any distance, and the water table relatively:
it rejected xmid 25.5 (0.5 m against 1.5 m, 67 %) and 100.5, 75 m from its left neighbours.

On made-up lines (the tests): five windows whose Vs agree pass. A window at 1.4 times the Vs
of its neighbours is rejected, and so is one with a water table 2.5 m deeper than the others'.
A window 1 m off, one step, passes. The same Vs window is kept when G4 found its curve
changing with one side, and one 40 steps from the others has none to compare with.
