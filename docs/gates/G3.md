# G3: curve QC, per window

Runs on each window's M0 pick, after the picking (an attempt of the `picking` stage in the QC
log, whose curve is saved in PAC's layout, `DispersionCurves_0000.csv`). The pick's metrics
(`paco.quality`), said in the gates' language, then the curve's own rules: the wavelength
range, mode jumps, the air wave, the trend, the points, the uncertainties. Code:
`src/paco/qc/g3_curve.py` over sigpipe's `measure_curve` (`sigpipe.masw.quality.curve`: PAC's
runs and curves picked by hand measured alike, a saved curve as a pick on its image through
`pick_of`; each measure of the curve, `of`); thresholds: `curve` in `qc_config.json`
(`CurveThresholds` over sigpipe's `CurveLimits`, with `QualityParameters`, over its
`PickLimits`, under `metrics`); the picking a run starts from: `picking` in the same file (as
far as the ridge holds, below); tests: `tests/test_qc_curve.py` and, for the metrics
themselves, `tests/test_quality.py`.

## What it checks

The first four are medians over the pick's tracked points (the image's columns); the rest look
at the curve saved for the inversion, resampled in wavelength, sorted by wavelength.

| Metric | Threshold (default) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `sharpness` | peak width at half its height above the floor, over a perfect plane wave's for the same window, frequency and velocity: at least 0.8 | A plane wave scores 1; well below it, the peak is a fringe or an edge, not a propagating wave: rejected, no parameter helps at this window length |
| `prominence` | peak height above the floor over its column's median height, over the same for a perfect plane wave (both counted up to 4): at least 0.5 | Barely above the rest of the image for what the window allows: the records muted to their surface waves (`muting {mute, 80, 1500}`) when they are not muted yet, else rejected. No filter: a filter common to the traces cannot change the image, which divides each trace's spectrum by its own amplitude |
| `on_data` | share of points on their column's brightest value (within 10 %): at least 0.6 | The tracker's smoothing drew the curve, not the data: competing ridges; narrow the band |
| `constant_wavelength` | share of points where velocity grows like frequency: at most 0.4 | The edge of what the window resolves, not a dispersion curve: cut the long wavelengths where that stretch starts (`max_wavelength`, in window lengths) |
| `n_points` | at least 2 | With no ridge kept: pick again with a looser mode rule (`mode_min_ratio` 1.2) |
| `aliased_points` | share of points below 2 x the receiver spacing: flagged (`aliasing_zone`), kept | The aliasing zone, where the ridge may be its alias; the picker follows a clear ridge down to one spacing |
| `beyond_reach_points` | share of points over 3 window lengths: flagged (`beyond_reach`), kept | Beyond the window's reach, where it resolves no velocity and the pick may be the tracker's; the picker follows its ridge as far as it holds |
| `curve_points` | the resampled curve's points: at least `min_points` (4) | Too few for a curve. When the kept ridge spans enough wavelengths, it is resampled finer (`wavelength_step`, down to 0.1 m, so that 4 points fit or more): a short window's ridge can span under 1 m and keep 1 point at the default 1 m; otherwise more of the ridge is kept (`min_relative_coherence` x 0.6) |
| `wavelength_ratio` | the curve's longest wavelength over its shortest: at least 1.34 | The inversion needs over 4/3 for its 3 layers, each at least a third of the shortest wavelength, down to half the longest: under it, `narrow_span` keeps more of the ridge (`min_relative_coherence` x 0.6), then rejects once the budget is spent. More points on a narrow span do not help: on `active_p2`, a curve of 9.2–11.2 m, resampled finer by `too_few_points`, still gives the inversion no parameters |
| `max_jump` | largest relative velocity step between consecutive points: at most 0.3 | Beyond it the pick jumped onto another mode: cut the band where the jump sits (the side with fewer points goes, `picking {"fmax"}` or `fmin`), then track with half the corridor |
| `air_wave_share` | share of points at 330–345 m/s: at most 0.5 | The air wave, not the ground: rejected. No mute parts it: one just under its speed cuts about 0.18 ms of it a metre from the shot, and every surface wave faster than it |
| `trend` | Spearman rank correlation of velocity with wavelength: negative is flagged | Normal dispersion has velocity rising with wavelength; the opposite can be a stiff layer over a softer one: flagged and kept (`Keep`), never rejected |
| `uncertainty` | median Lorentzian uncertainty over the velocity, over the points that have one: reported | The array's resolving power (receivers, spacing), the same for every window of a line. The picker caps each point's at 0.4 of its velocity, so a limit of 0.5 never fails, and one of 0.35 would leave out 11 of the 69 windows of a run of `active_p2`; G5's depth informed judges what a loose curve does to the model |
| `near_offset` | the distance from the window's nearest shot to its receivers (from `window.json`): at least half the longest wavelength kept | Closer, the long wavelengths may read slow (near field). Reported and kept (`near_field`): S2 already leaves out a window's shots nearer than half its trial curves' longest wavelength where the window has a farther one (`S2_rules.md`); a window with near shots only keeps them, and a curve may reach longer wavelengths than the trial curves |

Verdict: `reject` when a flag is not fixable (sharpness, the air wave, prominence on records
muted already or on a passive line), `retry` when every flag can be acted on, `pass` with no
flag or kept flags only. A change asked of an earlier stage (the records' mute, the phase
shift's band for `on_data`) is done once by PACo at the end of `pick`, then the window is
rejected if it still asks (`redone_once`, `loop.md`). `kept`: the curve's band and wavelength
range within the limit, its points there (the resampled curve, 5 to 10 points for a
24-receiver window: what the inversion gets, not the 110 to 150 tracked columns), the window's
receivers. A `qc_config.json` saved by an older version still reads: its `air_wave_mute_vmax`
and `max_uncertainty` are dropped.

## The pick, followed to both ends

The picker searches down to one spacing, and once it has its pick, follows the pick's ridge
past both its ends, a lower maximum taking its place only when brighter (a dimmer one is its
sidelobe or its alias). G3 flags the points under 2 spacings (`aliasing_zone`) and over 3
window lengths (`beyond_reach`), both kept, and PAC draws the two limits as λmin and λmax.
Measured on a run of `active_p2` (windows of 7 receivers every 1.5 m, 9 m long, 90 windows),
against the longest continuous run alone, searched down to 2 spacings: each pick contains that
run (the same ridge), and its first few points at the low end lie on the ridge, where the run
bends down towards the maxima under it.

- The high end reaches the grid's top, 100 Hz (median; lowest 74), against 68.7 Hz (lowest 58)
  for a search stopping at 2 spacings; 47 windows have points under 2 spacings.
- The low end reaches 10.0 Hz (median; quartiles 7.0-11.0), against 10.7 Hz (9.1-13.6) for the
  run alone, whose lowest ridge drops onto faint lines at 1 to 4 spacings (xmid_45: 8 Hz
  against 27.3).
- 83 windows have points over 3 window lengths, a median 63 % of their curve's points (one a
  metre of wavelength): where the ridge climbs out of the grid under 15 Hz.
- G3 passes all 90, against 86 and 4 retries (too few points, a narrow span) for the run
  alone. A point on a limit counts as within it: a curve resampled every metre holds one at
  exactly 3 m on a 1.5 m spacing, which its float32 values put at 2.9999998.

On the demo's first 24-receiver window the pick spans 16.5-46.5 Hz (16.5-33 Hz for the run
alone), up to the 50 Hz mains line. Followed from the ridge's widest stretch instead, the pick
there would be the branch above that line (56.5 to 78.5 Hz, 222 m/s).

## The pick, as far as its ridge holds

The pick is its longest continuous run (a step steeper than |d ln v / d ln f| = 2, or over
2 Hz of columns too weak to keep, ends it), with no cut at the long end, and keeps no point
where a perfect plane wave for the window stands less than 1 % above its column's median (the
window resolves no velocity there); sharpness and prominence are judged against a plane wave.
Measured on `active_p1`, windows every 4 receivers (figure: `figures/active_p1_short_picks.png`):

- every length's pick is smooth from about 15 to 45 Hz; below 12-15 Hz it scatters, above the
  50 Hz mains line (a dark column: the hum is in phase on every geophone, so its peak sits at
  the grid's top velocity) it follows several branches (`figures/active_p1_dense_picks.png`,
  24 receivers every 4);
- the continuity ends the picks at 12.5 to 16 Hz (medians) on 5 to 11 receivers, 10.5 Hz on 24,
  and at 43 to 45 Hz at the high end; with the contrast floor, the lowest points are 8.5 to
  10 Hz on every length;
- the measured sharpness and prominence equal a perfect plane wave's (ratio 1.00) at every
  length; fixed limits (prominence 2 of the column's median, sharpness 0.8 of the resolvable
  width) would fail every window of 5 to 11 receivers, whose plane wave only reaches a
  prominence of 1.06 to 1.2.

The ladder's 27 trials (`S2_rules.md`) pass 17 at 5 receivers, 20 at 7 and 9, 23 at 11 and 26
at 16; processed whole, the line gives curves on 65, 68, 77, 85 and 98 % of its windows, the
failures mostly within about 3 m of each shot. On the four 24-receiver windows every 24, every
curve passes (xmid 2.88 alone keeps a mild inverse trend, -0.14): xmid 14.88's mode jump sits
above the 50 Hz line, where the pick stops. With 16 receivers on the whole line, 79 of 81 curves
pass G3 and G4.

## On analytic images (the tests)

- a clean ridge: pass, a resampled curve of about 23 points at 5 to 27 m, uncertainties 5 to
  50 %, the curve rules all passed;
- a ridge 0.3 times as wide as the window resolves (0.28 of a plane wave's width): `sharpness`,
  rejected;
- a ridge 0.8 above a flat background of 0.6 (a prominence of 1.33, a third of the 4 a
  48-receiver plane wave counts as): `prominence` -> `muting {mute, 80, 1500}`; its records
  muted already: rejected;
- a brighter ridge above M0: `on_data` -> `dispersion {fmin 10, fmax 40}` (the pick's band);
- a ridge at a constant 6 m wavelength: `constant_wavelength` -> `picking {max_wavelength
  0.12}` (the stretch starts at 6 m, 0.12 of the 47 m window);
- no pick: `no_ridge` -> `picking {mode_min_ratio 1.2}`;
- a curve 60 % faster from 30.5 Hz on: `mode_jump` -> `picking {fmax 30.2}` (the band cut
  where the jump sits, the side with fewer points going); the band already cut: `corridor 0.1`;
- a curve at 330 to 345 m/s: `air_wave`, rejected, muted records or not;
- a curve falling from 200 m/s at 20 m to 433 m/s at 11 m: `inverse_dispersion`, kept, pass;
- a curve of 3 points: `too_few_points` -> `min_relative_coherence 0.3`;
- uncertainties of 60 % of the velocity, three points without one: 0.6 reported, no flag;
- a shot 0.75 m from a window whose curve reaches 27.85 m: `near_field`, kept, pass; 20 m away,
  or no geometry given: nothing.

## Design notes

G3 asks for no retry that could not change the curve, and holds no limit that cannot fail.

- `max_jump` is judged on the resampled curve: there good curves step by 26 % at most, against
  31 and 53 % on jumps; on the tracked points, good windows step by up to 47 %.
- At least 4 points a pick, and a pick with fewer changes something when retried: on
  `active_p2`'s windows of 5 receivers, ridges of 22 frequencies span under 1 m of wavelength
  and keep 1 point at the 1 m step, which lowering the coherence rule twice does not help; the
  retry resamples finer first.
- The window length is the line's, proposed by the ladder and chosen by the agent: sharpness
  rejects a window, without a length change of its own.
- An earlier stage G3 blames (the records' mute, the phase shift's band) is done once, by PACo
  at the end of `pick`: left to the agent, it is not run, and the window ends without a curve,
  unsaid.
